# PIT Phase 9A.1 — transaction-bound payment campaign

**Status: the approved 15-transaction campaign has executed on Base Sepolia; no further writes are authorized.** The six historical contracts retain their Sourcify matches. The two new payment routers are deployed; their source publication remains pending separate authorization.

## Approved artifact and execution boundary

`deployments/base-sepolia-paid-bound-candidate.json` is the sole campaign input. `scripts/operations/pit9a1-bound-plan.mjs` generates it with recursively sorted JSON keys, UTF-8, no insignificant whitespace and no trailing newline. Its SHA-256 is the authorization identifier. Every action has a SHA-256 of its complete specification. Immediately before signing, the executor derives the complete calldata or creation bytecode, hashes the exact payload including sender, chain, nonce, destination, value, gas ceiling and fee ceiling, signs with the existing encrypted PIT keystore, recovers the signer from the signed bytes, then checks those bytes against the approved payload. The script has no arbitrary RPC or manifest override for broadcast.

The authorized external write mode was one explicit `--testnet-step INDEX --approval-file /tmp/FILE` at a time. The approval file must be mode 0600 and contain exactly `GO PIT9A TESTNET <manifest SHA-256>`. This file was a local execution gate, used only after the matching operator approval in the conversation. The 15 approved steps are consumed; the GO must not be reused. `--testnet-preflight` and `--testnet-recover` are read-only; `--local-rehearsal`, `--local-audit`, `--local-recover` and `--local-check-manifest` use only loopback Anvil or local files. A pending or uncertain send blocks the next step; no automatic resend exists.

The three time-sensitive parameters are deterministic functions of prior canonical receipt timestamps: each paid quote expires 900 seconds after its preceding approval; the lock unlocks 3,600 seconds after its asset approval; vesting starts 3,600 seconds after its asset approval, cliffs at 7,200 seconds, and lasts 86,400 seconds. The exact dependency index and offset are in the manifest. If a quote expires before the next operation, or the timing no longer suits the operator, the campaign halts and needs a new manifest and approval. These are binding derivations, not open-ended placeholders.

## Financial and recovery policy

Maximum gas price: **0.05 gwei**; aggregate ETH ceiling: **0.0011 test ETH**; 15 per-action gas ceilings total **14,990,000 gas**. Reserving every ceiling at the maximum gas price plus 0.00002 ETH L1/operator allowance per transaction totals **0.0010495 ETH**, leaving **0.0000505 ETH** margin below the aggregate ceiling. Live gas and Base L1/operator estimates are rechecked before each signature. The executor records a pending hash before sending, requires canonical successful receipts and two Base Sepolia confirmations, checks fee and operation events, exact Circle transfer, roles, schedules, token balances and zero residual allowances, then writes a mode-0600 JSON evidence state. A timeout, replacement, noncanonical receipt, unexpected nonce, changed bytecode, event mismatch or budget breach halts the campaign. A reverted transaction consumes gas but retains no atomic service fee.

Before the live campaign, the full 15-step executor completed on a **local Anvil fork**, with real encrypted-keystore signatures, receipts and state readbacks: 11,731,819 gas units used, 0.000050068041499838 local test ETH charged, 0.42 Circle test USDC transferred within the fork, five fee events. Those fork transactions were not live Base Sepolia transactions. The fork's gas price and L1 accounting are not a live cost quote. Targeted tests mutate beneficiaries, cap, roles, recipients, spenders, amounts, bytecode, chain, signer, nonce, signed bytes, budgets and recovery states; the executor's no-sign CLI rejects an altered manifest.

The initial read-only preflight observed nonce 19, 0.001954776660523391 test ETH and 20 test USDC. Those preflight values were checked again before each approved write and are historical. The six historical Sourcify matches remain separate from the two new, unpublished payment routers. Staging remains undeployed; `workers_dev: false` cannot provide a `workers.dev` URL.

The remaining non-technical risk is that this is an internal review and testnet trial, not an independent audit or production merchant approval. No production fees, mainnet writes, public staging deployment or manual MetaMask tests are included.
