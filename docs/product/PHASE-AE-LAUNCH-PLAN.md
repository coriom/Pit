# PIT Phase AE — product hypothesis and invitation plan

**Proposed positioning:** **PIT — Understand your DeFi positions and costs.** Explore simple, privacy-conscious tools to analyze a manually described portfolio, estimate transaction costs and understand DeFi tradeoffs. This is a product hypothesis, not evidence of demand or a claim that PIT is an exchange, automatic scanner, or comprehensive risk platform.

The first audience is independent DeFi users seeking simple portfolio, cost and position-risk analysis. Testnet traders are secondary because the execution lab is constrained to reviewed testnets and Uniswap execution is unaccepted. Tax/accounting evaluators are secondary because Taxlot integration and legacy recovery have separate open acceptance requirements. The three first-use journeys are Portfolio Exposure Analyzer, Swap Cost Simulator, and Impermanent Loss Calculator. Six wallet-free calculators form the proposed read-only candidate; the full toolbox also contains public-chain lookup, wallet and testnet lab tools with different access requirements.

## Small invited experiment — proposed, not launched

Invite 10–20 testers after a separate public-release approval, and aim for at least five structured usability conversations. Recruit individually from relevant DeFi learning, LP and self-custody communities where invitations are permitted. Start with an opt-in private invitation, publish educational examples only after review, then assess whether broader posts are warranted. No automated outreach, scraped audiences, incentives tied to transactions, or unapproved analytics.

Ask each participant to: (1) find the relevant calculator from the homepage; (2) complete the illustrative portfolio exposure example; (3) explain why an unpriced holding makes total coverage incomplete; (4) compare two manually supplied swap-cost scenarios; and (5) explain assumed versus actual values. Observe the impermanent-loss example as an optional sixth task. Do not ask for actual holdings, wallet addresses, CSV files, credentials or screenshots of private balances.

Track only consented session notes: whether the homepage promise is understood in the participant's own words; seconds to first useful result; each task completed without guidance (yes/no); where confusion or abandonment occurs; and self-reported willingness to reuse or recommend a tool. Targets for the first review, not forecasts: 4/5 participants understand the tool-first message, 4/5 complete at least one flagship journey unaided, and median first useful result under three minutes. A small sample cannot establish conversion, retention or market demand. No client telemetry is installed. Aggregate counts manually, retain no sensitive input values, and let participants opt out of note-taking.

Feedback template: participant code and consent; device/browser; task; completion without help; elapsed time; observed confusion in the participant's words; one desired improvement; whether they would reuse/recommend and why; severity; reproducibility; follow-up permission. Avoid recording monetary inputs, portfolio contents, addresses and uploaded files. Prioritize issues by **blocker / high / medium / low**, then by number of participants affected and whether the fix protects correct interpretation or privacy. Treat comprehension and economic misinterpretation issues before cosmetic requests.

## Draft copy — unpublished

**Initial announcement.** “PIT is preparing a small read-only beta of browser-local DeFi tools. Start with manual portfolio exposure, swap-cost scenarios or impermanent-loss examples. These are estimates based on your inputs, not live prices or investment advice. We are looking for a small group willing to test whether the tools are clear.”

**Portfolio post.** “A position list can look complete while a single unpriced holding makes allocation percentages misleading. PIT's illustrative portfolio example shows known value separately from incomplete coverage. Enter your own prices only if you choose; nothing is discovered from a wallet.”

**Swap-cost post.** “A quote may already include venue costs while wallet gas remains separate. PIT's local simulator asks you to name the assumptions, so a missing approval or gas estimate makes scenarios non-comparable instead of producing a false winner.”

**Impermanent-loss post.** “Compare a fee-free 50/50 pool with holding the same assets using a simple price-change example. PIT shows the modeled difference and makes clear that fees, rewards, depegs and real execution are outside the model.”

**Invitation.** “Would you spend 15 minutes testing three PIT tools and telling us where the wording or results confuse you? Use only illustrative data. No wallet connection, personal portfolio or transaction is requested. Participation is optional; please reply privately if interested.”

**Questionnaire.** “What did you think PIT helps you do? Which tool did you find first? Could you get a useful result without help? Which assumption or limitation was unclear? Would you use the tool again or recommend it, and why? What single change would matter most?”

Potential future economics to evaluate separately: optional paid advanced analysis/reporting, and execution/routing economics subject to legal review. This plan activates no payment, subscription, referral fee or trading commission. Public origin, headers, browser QA, legal language and release authorization are prerequisites before invitations or posts are sent.
