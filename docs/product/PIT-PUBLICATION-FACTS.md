# PIT local toolbox publication facts (Phase BH)

This is a release checklist for the isolated 119-task, 20-workspace static toolbox. It does not authorize publication. The protected Production deployment remains on its prior package. The private build stays `noindex,nofollow`; the public build requires `PIT_TOOLBOX_PUBLIC_RELEASE_APPROVED=yes`, an approved HTTPS origin, completed legal/privacy notices, and a separate deployment decision. Its sitemap contains only the homepage, catalogue, 20 workspaces and 113 focused tasks. Trading, wallet and accounting routes are outside that build.

## Facts verified on 2026-10-22

- The `corioms-projects` Vercel team readback reports `billing.plan: hobby`. Vercel limits Hobby to personal, non-commercial use; commercial use requires a different authorized hosting/plan decision. Sources: [terms](https://vercel.com/legal/terms), [fair use](https://vercel.com/docs/limits/fair-use-guidelines), [Hobby plan](https://vercel.com/docs/plans/hobby). Do not purchase or upgrade from this checklist.
- Any hosting provider receives normal request metadata, including IP and requested path. For the existing protected Vercel host, its one-hour Hobby **runtime log dashboard** retention is not a promise that all provider request metadata is deleted after one hour. Static delivery, security, authentication and build records can have different handling. Sources: [Vercel privacy notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice), [runtime logs](https://vercel.com/docs/logs/runtime), [limits](https://vercel.com/docs/limits). Provider-wide retention/deletion criteria must be checked for the final selected host rather than invented.
- For a France-based **professional** publisher, government guidance distinguishes a natural person (name, contact, address) from a company (registered name, legal form, capital, contact, address), with host details and applicable registration, VAT, editorial and regulated-activity details. This is jurisdiction-conditional, not a universal template. Source: [French Ministry of the Economy](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/entreprises/developper-son-entreprise/innover-et-numeriser-son-entreprise/mentions-sur-votre-site-internet-les-obligations-respecter).
- Where GDPR Article 13 applies, the notice must identify the controller and contact, processing purpose and legal basis, recipients, retention period or criteria, relevant rights, and applicable international-transfer information. Sources: [GDPR Article 13](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679), [CNIL guidance](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/conformite-rgpd-information-des-personnes-et-transparence).

## Operator inputs needed before replacing publication-pending notices

1. Decide and document whether publication is genuinely personal/non-commercial or professional/commercial, the publisher's operating jurisdiction and intended audience. Confirm Hobby eligibility for the chosen model. A professional/commercial model needs a separately authorized compliant hosting plan or provider.
2. Supply the real publisher identity/status and working contact. Supply any address, company registration, VAT, editorial or regulated-activity details required in the chosen jurisdiction. Do not infer these from a GitHub or Vercel account.
3. Review the privacy notice against the actual setup: controller/contact, purpose and basis where applicable, Vercel as hosting recipient, retention **criteria** for operator-held data, rights/contact channel, international transfers and provider metadata limits. Do not state an unverified provider-wide deletion period. Approve final legal wording for the chosen audience.
4. Choose the exact public HTTPS origin, indexing policy and separate public-deployment authorization. Remove the `data-publication-pending` markers only when the corresponding details are actually supplied and reviewed; the public package validator rejects them until then.

The local-toolbox execution model has no app analytics, wallet, RPC, account or server-side financial-input collection. Its browser calculations require JavaScript. CSP now blocks form submission as a defense against accidental GET navigation with sensitive inputs when calculation scripts are unavailable.
